SALES PERFORMANCE & CUSTOMER INSIGHTS
DATACAMP CAPSTONE PROJECT

This project examines sales performance and customer purchasing behavior using Python.

Four datasets were analyzed: customers, orders, order items, and products.

Data cleaning was performed using pandas to improve data quality and consistency.

Duplicate records, missing values, inconsistent formats, and data types were addressed.

Primary keys and foreign key relationships were validated before data integration.

The cleaned datasets were combined into a consolidated sales fact table.

Key performance indicators included total revenue, total orders, and Average Order Value (AOV).

Monthly revenue trends were analyzed to understand sales performance over time.

Product and category revenue analyses were conducted to identify top-performing items.

Regional sales and customer spending patterns were examined.

Customer purchasing behavior and order statuses were evaluated.

An interactive Streamlit dashboard was developed to visualize the analysis and support business decision-making.
